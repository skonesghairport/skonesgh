# Skonesgh App | Security, Learning & Aviation Awareness

## Slide 1 — Skonesgh App
### A practical field guide for sharper security practice

**Skones Security**

The Skonesgh app brings together security learning resources, operational awareness, and official aviation quick views in one focused workspace.

---

## Slide 2 — What the app provides

- A clean public launchpad for cybersecurity education and operational awareness.
- A free-first resource directory with provider, level, credential-path, and keyword filters.
- Official FlightRadar24 links for global traffic and airport-specific quick views.
- Clear safety boundaries: no private course content, credentials, FlightRadar24 scraping, or mirrored provider data.
- A new Skones Security College and Online Education experience for guided learning paths.

## Slide 4 — Top free-first cybersecurity resources

### 1. Cisco: Introduction to Cybersecurity
A strong starting point for privacy, threats, vulnerabilities, and everyday defensive habits. The listing describes free learning access, approximately six hours of material, and seven labs.

### 2. Microsoft Learn: Describe the concepts of cybersecurity
A beginner learning path covering threats, cryptography, networks, devices, and application security with no prerequisites.

### 3. Cisco: Cybersecurity Essentials
A foundation course that supports preparation for the entry-level CCST Cybersecurity certification; the exam remains separate.

### 4. NIST NICE Online Learning Directory
A government-maintained directory for finding additional free and low-cost learning content. Credential conditions vary by provider.

> Free learning access does not necessarily mean a free certificate or exam. The app labels these distinctions so learners can verify current provider terms before enrolling.

---

## Slide 5 — Search and filter workflow

1. Enter a keyword such as “beginner,” “labs,” or “certificate.”
2. Narrow results by provider, learning level, or credential path.
3. Review the resource card’s format and credential note.
4. Open the official provider link in a new tab.
5. Re-check enrollment, trial, certificate, and exam conditions on the provider site.

The filters are designed to support comparison without embedding or reproducing third-party course content.

---

## Slide 6 — Regional airline filter

The FlightRadar24 panel includes a region selector for **North America, Europe, Middle East, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, Africa,** and **All regions**.

When a region is selected, the app filters the representative airline/airport quick-view set to matching locations. The selection updates the visible quick-view buttons and keeps the context focused on the user’s chosen region.

The filter is a navigation aid, not a live data feed. It does not claim to calculate airline schedules or replicate FlightRadar24 coverage.

---

## Slide 7 — FlightRadar24 quick views

- **Global map** opens official worldwide traffic.
- Airport buttons open official FlightRadar24 pages for locations such as ATL, JFK, LHR, DXB, HND, SIN, SYD, GRU, and JNB.
- Each quick view provides a recognizable airport code, location label, and official destination.
- The selected quick view becomes the active context in the panel.
- External pages open in a new tab, leaving Skonesgh available for continued reference.

> Provider-owned aviation data stays with FlightRadar24. Skonesgh supplies a curated launchpad and does not scrape, mirror, or bypass access controls.

## Slide 8 — Skones Security College & Online Education

The new education tab is credited to **Skones Security** and organizes learning into three guided tracks:

- **Security Foundations:** privacy, threats, vulnerabilities, and defensive habits.
- **Operational Readiness:** connect cyber hygiene to physical-security operations, reporting, and escalation.
- **Certification Pathways:** compare learning access with separate exam, certificate, or credential conditions.

The tab links to official Cisco, Microsoft Learn, and NIST NICE resources while preserving the full searchable directory on the Resources route.

---

## Slide 9 — Safe, transparent resource use

Skonesgh clearly distinguishes:

- Local editorial guidance from provider-owned course material.
- Free learning access from paid certificates, exams, and trials.
- Official external links from copied or scraped content.
- Aviation awareness links from live aviation data ownership.

This model keeps the app useful for discovery while respecting provider terms, privacy, and access boundaries.

Skonesgh helps security-minded users discover trustworthy learning paths and reach official aviation context quickly, while keeping the boundaries around third-party content and data explicit.
