# Security resources research

Checked 2026-08-22.

| Resource | Official source | Verified facts used in the UI |
|---|---|---|
| FlightRadar24 | https://www.flightradar24.com/ | Official live flight tracker; the app links out rather than scraping or embedding private data. |
| Coursera cybersecurity catalog | https://www.coursera.org/courses?query=cybersecurity | Coursera describes free previews for many courses and a 7-day trial for eligible programs; certificates and full access generally require paid access or approved financial aid. |
| Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity | https://skillsforall.com/course/introduction-to-cybersecurity | Official Cisco Skills for All listing describes a free beginner course with about 6 hours and 7 labs. Certificate/completion-record availability should be confirmed after sign-in. |
| Cisco Cybersecurity Essentials | https://skillsforall.com/course/cybersecurity-essentials?courseLang=en-US | Official Cisco listing says the course prepares learners for entry-level CCST Cybersecurity; the certification exam is separate. |
| Microsoft Learn cybersecurity fundamentals | https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/describe-basic-concepts-of-cybersecurity/ | Free learning path with no prerequisites; covers threats, cryptography, network, device, and application security. The official page shows an achievement-code option; certification is separate. |
| ISC2 CC transition page | https://www.isc2.org/landing/1mcc | ISC2 says the One Million Certified in Cybersecurity program closed new enrollment on May 20, 2026. Previously issued course access and exam codes may remain valid, with stated deadlines; standard CC exam and maintenance fees apply outside the concluded free program. |
| NIST NICE learning directory | https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/resources/online-learning-content | NIST maintains a directory of free and low-cost cybersecurity learning content and warns that credential status varies by provider. |

The app labels each resource’s access and credential conditions explicitly and links to the official provider page. It does not claim that every free course awards a free certificate.
