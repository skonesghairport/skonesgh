# Verified Ghana Aviation Training Sources

## Ghana Airports Company Limited (GACL)
Source: https://www.gacl.com.gh/aviation-security-training-school/

The GACL Aviation Security Training School page states that the school was established in response to ICAO requirements and initially trained AVSEC officers. It says the school is managed by Ghana Airports Company Limited after the 2007 decoupling exercise. The page states that the scope expanded to include private security agencies and non-security staff, and that additional training assistance may be offered upon request. It describes the facility as located near GACL headquarters at the airport and notes certified national instructors and ICAO-related training experience.

Use in Skonesgh: list this as an official external Ghana aviation-security training reference. Do not claim Skones Security is affiliated with GACL or that GACL courses are free unless an official source states that.

## Ghana Civil Aviation Training Academy (GATA), Ghana Civil Aviation Authority
Source: https://www.gcaa.com.gh/gata/gata-courses/

The official GATA courses page lists aviation-related categories including ICAO Trainair Plus, engineering, resource management, CBTA dangerous goods, safety management, flight dispatch, IATA, aeronautical radio operator, quality training, air traffic control, security training, English courses, and project management professional. The page states that GATA is located at Kotoka International Airport, Accra, and provides official contact details.

Use in Skonesgh: list GATA as an official Ghana aviation education provider and link to its course catalog. Treat pricing, enrollment, schedule, and credential terms as provider-controlled and time-sensitive.

## Accuracy and disclosure rules

Skones Security College may curate pathways and publish original orientation material. It must not present GACL, GATA, ICAO, GCAA, Ghana Immigration Service, G4S, ASIS, or another organization as a Skones Security affiliate or partner without a written agreement and an official public confirmation. Use labels such as “official external resource,” “partnership opportunity,” or “requires provider confirmation.”


## G4S Academy and accredited training
Sources: https://www.g4s.com/en-za/what-we-do/g4s-academy and https://www.g4s.com/en-gb/what-we-do/employment-services/accredited-security-training

G4S describes its Academy as an internal platform for sharing knowledge with customers, suppliers, partners, and stakeholders. Its United Kingdom training page describes employer/training-provider programs such as security guarding, door supervision, CCTV operations, and close protection, including SIA-related qualifications in the UK context. These are not Ghana-specific free courses, and the page includes paid or qualification-dependent conditions.

Use in Skonesgh: reference G4S only as an official external industry-learning or benchmarking source. Do not copy G4S modules, imply a G4S–Skones partnership, or market a G4S course as available through Skones Security without written authorization from G4S.

## ASIS International education
Source: https://www.asisonline.org/professional-development/education/

ASIS International’s official education page describes webinars, certificate courses, executive education, certification preparation, and preferred CPE providers. The page also describes free content through ASIS Upskill and paid or credential-linked programs, including security project management, crisis management, and enterprise security risk management examples.

Use in Skonesgh: list ASIS as an official external professional-development reference, with separate labels for free content, paid courses, CPEs, and certification preparation. Do not represent ASIS as an affiliate, exchange partner, or credential issuer for Skones Security.

## Partnership and exchange wording

Cross-deployment, exchange programs, international private-security partnerships, and airport-to-airport training should be shown as “partnership opportunities” or “future pathways” until a written agreement is signed and publicly confirmed by each organization. No current Skones Security affiliation with GACL, GATA, G4S, ASIS International, ICAO, immigration authorities, or an international airport was verified in this research pass.


## Ghana Immigration Service and border-management reference
Source: https://gis.gov.gh/gis-begin-shift-sponsored-training-in-risk-analysis-and-intelligence-led-border-management/

The Ghana Immigration Service page reports a 10-day training program on risk analysis and intelligence collection under the SHIFT project, implemented with ICMPD. The article describes intelligence-led border management, data analysis, suspicious-behavior detection, and inter-agency collaboration. It identifies this as a government/partner capacity-building initiative for selected frontline officers, not a public Skones course.

Use in Skonesgh: create an “immigration and border-management context” reference link. Do not imply GIS enrollment, endorsement, or partnership with Skones Security.

## ICAO Aviation Security Training Packages
Source: https://www.icao.int/isd-security/packages

ICAO’s official catalog describes aviation-security packages including Basic Aviation Security, Airport Security Supervisors, Air Cargo and Mail Security, Aviation Security National Inspectors, Aviation Security Managers, and Behaviour Detection. The catalog describes target audiences, prerequisites, and typical five- or seven-day course durations for several packages, and provides an ICAO contact for course information.

Use in Skonesgh: present these as international official aviation-security training references. Do not reproduce restricted course materials or claim that Skones Security delivers ICAO training.
