# Skonesgh App: Security, Learning & Aviation Awareness
## Presenter Script

### Slide 1 — Skonesgh App: Security, Learning & Aviation Awareness
Welcome to the Skonesgh app, a practical learning and awareness workspace presented by Skones Security. The product brings together free-first cybersecurity education, operational-security orientation, regional airline context, and official FlightRadar24 launch points. The central idea is simple: help learners discover legitimate resources and make responsible next-step decisions without pretending to own external courses, aviation data, or third-party credentials.

### Slide 2 — What the app provides
The app is organized around three actions: learn, orient, and verify. Users can browse security foundations, connect cyber hygiene to operational readiness, and open official external resources when they are ready to continue. Aviation awareness is intentionally presented as a navigation aid, not as a replacement for airport systems, airline operations, immigration services, or provider-owned data.

### Slide 3 — Resource directory at a glance
The resource directory compares provider, level, access model, and credential notes. That makes an important distinction visible: free learning access does not always mean a free examination or certificate. Provider terms can change, so the app directs learners to the official source before enrollment, payment, or sharing personal information.

### Slide 4 — Top free-first cybersecurity resources
The strongest free-first starting points are Cisco Skills for All’s [Introduction to Cybersecurity](https://skillsforall.com/course/introduction-to-cybersecurity), Microsoft Learn’s [basic cybersecurity concepts path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/describe-basic-concepts-of-cybersecurity/), and the [NIST NICE online-learning directory](https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/resources/online-learning-content). These resources support foundational learning, practical orientation, and broader discovery. The app does not claim that a completion badge or course automatically creates a professional license.

### Slide 5 — Search and filter workflow
The workflow is designed to reduce noise. A learner searches by topic, narrows by level or provider, checks whether access is free, paid, or provider-confirmed, and then opens the official source. The app keeps third-party content external rather than scraping course pages or copying proprietary material. This is especially important for credentials, trials, and enrollment requirements.

### Slide 6 — Regional airline filter
The regional airline filter is a context tool. It lets a user switch among regions such as North America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and Africa. The interface then displays representative airline and airport context associated with the selected region. It is not an airline booking tool, a live operational feed, or an immigration decision system.

### Slide 7 — FlightRadar24 quick views
Flight quick views are curated launchpads. Selecting an airport opens an official FlightRadar24 map or airport page in a new tab. Skonesgh does not scrape, mirror, or bypass FlightRadar24 data. Users should treat the external provider’s information, availability, account requirements, and terms as authoritative for the service itself.

### Slide 8 — Skones Security College & Online Education
The College experience provides Skones Security’s original orientation tracks: Security Foundations, Operational Readiness, and Certification Pathways. It also connects learners to official external courses from Cisco, Microsoft, and NIST NICE. The page is designed to help someone choose a starting point, understand prerequisites, and separate learning access from formal certification.

### Slide 9 — Safe, transparent resource use
Transparency is part of the product design. Skones Security owns the app’s curation and original orientation content. External organizations own their courses, data, pricing, access conditions, exams, certificates, and credentials. The app therefore uses labels such as official external resource, provider confirmation required, and affiliate link pending agreement. It does not claim an endorsement or partnership that has not been publicly confirmed.

### Slide 10 — Learn deliberately. Observe responsibly.
The app is intended to support careful learning and responsible awareness. It is not a substitute for official airport security training, government immigration guidance, licensed security instruction, or provider-specific certification. The best next step is to use the app to identify a legitimate source, verify its current terms, and then continue through the source’s own official process.

### Slide 11 — Ghana aviation and border-security pathways
For Ghana-focused learners, the app points to the [GACL Aviation Security Training School](https://www.gacl.com.gh/aviation-security-training-school/), whose public page describes aviation-security training and a scope that includes private-security agencies and non-security staff. It also references the [Ghana Civil Aviation Training Academy course catalog](https://www.gcaa.com.gh/gata/gata-courses/), which lists aviation categories including security, safety management, dangerous goods, flight dispatch, and air-traffic control. The [Ghana Immigration Service SHIFT announcement](https://gis.gov.gh/gis-begin-shift-sponsored-training-in-risk-analysis-and-intelligence-led-border-management/) is included as government context about risk analysis and intelligence-led border management, not as a public Skones course. Finally, [ICAO’s aviation-security packages](https://www.icao.int/isd-security/packages) provide international reference points such as basic AVSEC, airport supervisors, cargo and mail security, inspectors, managers, and behaviour detection. None of these references should be read as a Skones affiliation.

### Slide 12 — International modules, exchanges, and affiliate boundaries
The G4S references in the app are external industry references, not Skones-delivered modules. Public G4S material describes areas such as security guarding, door supervision, CCTV operations, and close protection, with market-specific training and qualification conditions; see the [G4S Academy](https://www.g4s.com/en-za/what-we-do/g4s-academy) and [G4S accredited training page](https://www.g4s.com/en-gb/what-we-do/employment-services/accredited-security-training). The app also points to [ASIS International education](https://www.asisonline.org/professional-development/education/), which includes webinars, certificate courses, executive education, certification preparation, and CPE pathways.

Exchange programs, cross-deployment, international private-security cooperation, and airport-to-airport partnerships are future opportunity concepts in the app. They are not active programs, job placements, immigration arrangements, or affiliate claims. Any future program would require written agreements, local licensing, airport authorization, safeguarding, provider confirmation, and a clearly defined responsibility for training, employment, insurance, and data protection.

## Closing delivery cue
Close by emphasizing that Skones Security can build the orientation layer and curate legitimate pathways, while the relevant airport, government, association, security firm, or education provider controls its own training and credentials. The app is strongest when it helps people make informed, lawful, and verifiable choices.
